# Fueling and Engine Safety

## Before fueling
Moor the boat securely, shut off engines, and extinguish all flames including galley stoves and cigarettes. Switch off electrical equipment and close all ports, hatches and doors so vapors cannot enter the cabin. Ask passengers to leave the boat and wait on the dock.

## During fueling
Keep the nozzle in contact with the fill opening to prevent static sparks. Do not top off the tank; leave about 10 percent of the tank empty for expansion. Wipe up any spills immediately and dispose of rags ashore, never overboard.

## After fueling
Open all hatches and run the bilge blower for at least 4 minutes before starting the engine. Sniff the bilge, especially low areas, for gasoline vapor. If you smell fuel, do not start the engine until you have found and fixed the source.

## Fire extinguishers
Carry at least one approved B-rated extinguisher on any vessel with an enclosed engine space or fuel tank. Check the pressure gauge monthly, and replace or service disposable extinguishers after 12 years.

## Carbon monoxide
Never let anyone sit or swim near the exhaust outlet or on the swim platform while the engine or generator is running. Carbon monoxide can kill in minutes and is invisible and odorless. Install a marine-rated CO alarm in any enclosed living space.
