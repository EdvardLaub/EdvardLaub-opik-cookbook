# Life Jackets (PFDs)

## Carriage requirements
Every vessel under the Marrowbay Waterways Code must carry one wearable life jacket per person aboard, in good condition and sized for the wearer. Jackets must be within reach, not stowed in sealed lockers or under cargo. Vessels longer than 12 metres must also carry one throwable flotation cushion or ring.

## Children
Anyone under 13 must wear a properly fitted life jacket at all times while the vessel is underway, including in the cabin. Test the fit by lifting the child by the shoulders of the jacket: the chin and ears should not slip through.

## Inspection and replacement
Inspect each jacket before every season. Replace it if the shell is torn, the buckles are cracked, or it floats noticeably lower than when new. Inflatable jackets need their cylinder weighed and their bladder checked annually; the Harbor Authority recommends replacing the inflation cartridge every 3 years.

## Wearing versus stowing
Carrying a jacket is the legal minimum, but most drowning cases involve jackets that were stowed, not worn. The handbook recommends wearing a jacket in open water, at night, in cold water, and whenever the vessel is moving faster than 15 knots.
