# Fermentation at Hollowbrook

## Temperature ranges
Ale yeast (the house strain is Hollowbrook Ale 11) ferments best between 18 and 20 C (64 to 68 F). Above 23 C it throws fruity esters and hot fusel alcohols. Lager yeast wants 9 to 12 C (48 to 54 F) for the first two weeks, followed by a diacetyl rest at 16 C for two days.

## Pitching and timeline
Pitch one packet of yeast per 20 litres of wort below 24 C. Expect visible airlock activity within 12 to 24 hours. Primary fermentation of a pale ale takes 7 to 10 days. Do not rely on the airlock to judge the end: take a hydrometer reading on day 7 and again two days later, and bottle only when the two readings match.

## Gravity targets
A pale ale that starts at 1.052 original gravity should finish between 1.010 and 1.012, which is roughly 5.5 percent alcohol by volume.
