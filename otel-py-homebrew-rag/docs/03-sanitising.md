# Cleaning and Sanitising

## Clean first, then sanitise
Sanitiser only works on clean surfaces. Soak equipment in a percarbonate cleaner at 50 C for 20 minutes and rinse well before sanitising. Never use scented dish soap on plastic fermenters; it leaves scratches and a perfume that ends up in the beer.

## No-rinse sanitiser
Hollowbrook uses an acid-based no-rinse sanitiser at 1 ml per litre of water. Contact time is 2 minutes. Foam is harmless and should not be rinsed off, because rinsing with tap water reintroduces microbes.

## What to sanitise
Sanitise everything that touches the beer after the boil: the fermenter, lid, airlock, thief, hydrometer, spoon, tubing and bottling wand. The boil itself sanitises the wort and anything left in the kettle.

## Signs of infection
A white, wrinkled film on the surface or a sour smell and ropey strands in the liquid means the batch is infected. Do not drink it. Bleach the fermenter and replace plastic tubing.
