# Mashing at Hollowbrook

## Strike water and mash temperature
The Hollowbrook house method mashes at 66 C (151 F) for 60 minutes for a balanced pale ale. Heat the strike water to 74 C (165 F) for a typical 3:1 liquor-to-grist ratio; the cold grain pulls it down to the target. Mashing at 64 C gives a drier, more fermentable wort, and 69 C leaves more body and residual sweetness.

## Checking conversion
Use the iodine test after 45 minutes: put a drop of wort on a white plate and add a drop of iodine. If it stays amber, starch conversion is finished. Blue-black means keep mashing for another 15 minutes.

## Sparging
Sparge with water at 77 C (170 F), never hotter. Water above 80 C pulls tannins from the husks and makes the beer astringent, like over-steeped tea. Stop collecting runnings when the gravity of the last runnings drops below 1.010.
