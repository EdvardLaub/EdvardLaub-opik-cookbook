# Marlowe Barista M2 Espresso Machine: Owner's Manual

This manual covers the Marlowe Barista M2 (model MB-M2), a single-boiler
espresso machine with a built-in conical burr grinder and a manual steam wand.

## 1. First setup

Rinse the water tank and fill it with fresh, cold water up to the MAX line.
Run a flush cycle before first use: place a container under the group head,
hold the BREW button for three seconds, and let 200 ml of water pass through.

The machine heats up in about 40 seconds. The READY light turns solid green
when the boiler has reached brew temperature.

## 2. Brewing espresso

The factory brew temperature is 93 degrees Celsius. You can change it in
steps of 1 degree between 88 and 96 degrees: hold the BREW button and the
STEAM button together for two seconds, then press STEAM to raise or BREW to
lower the temperature.

For a double shot, dose 18 grams of ground coffee into the double basket and
tamp with about 15 kilograms of pressure. A good double shot takes 25 to 30
seconds and yields roughly 36 grams of espresso. If the shot runs faster than
20 seconds, grind finer; if it takes longer than 35 seconds, grind coarser.

The built-in grinder has 15 settings. Setting 1 is the finest and setting 15
is the coarsest. The factory default is setting 6.

## 3. Steaming milk

Press STEAM and wait for the READY light to blink, which means the boiler has
reached steam temperature. The switch from brew to steam takes about 15 seconds.
Purge the wand for two seconds before and after every use, and wipe it with a
damp cloth immediately after steaming so milk does not bake on.

Never leave the steam wand submerged in milk for more than 60 seconds
without moving the pitcher.

## 4. Cleaning and descaling

Backflush the group head with plain water once a week. Use the supplied blind
basket and a cleaning tablet once a month.

Descale the machine every 3 months if you use tap water, or every 6 months if
you use filtered water. Use only the Marlowe descaling solution or a citric
acid solution of 20 grams per litre. Never use vinegar, because it can damage
the rubber seals. The DESCALE light turns solid orange when a descaling cycle
is due.

To start a descaling cycle, fill the tank with the solution, hold the STEAM
and BREW buttons for five seconds, and wait for the cycle to finish. The
cycle takes about 25 minutes. Afterwards, rinse the tank and run two full
tanks of fresh water through the machine.

## 5. Troubleshooting

If the pump is loud and no water comes out, the tank is probably empty or not
seated correctly. Refill the tank and press it firmly into place.

If the READY light blinks red, the boiler is overheating. Switch the machine
off, let it cool for 30 minutes, and switch it on again. If the red light
returns, contact support.

If the grinder stops mid-cycle, the hopper may contain a foreign object. Unplug
the machine before you inspect the hopper.

## 6. Warranty and support

The Marlowe Barista M2 carries a two-year limited warranty on parts and labour
from the date of purchase. The warranty does not cover damage caused by
descaling with vinegar, and it does not cover wear on the grinder burrs.

Register your machine within 30 days of purchase to receive an extra six months
of coverage. Support is available on weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00 Central
European Time.
