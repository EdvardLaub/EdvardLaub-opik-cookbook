# Marrow Lane Community Garden: Planting Calendar

## Frost dates
The average last spring frost at Marrow Lane is 20 April, and the average first autumn frost is 25 October. Our site is in a low spot, so frost pockets can arrive up to a week later in spring. Tender plants such as tomatoes, courgettes and runner beans go outside only after 15 May.

## Sow in February and March
Under cover on the windowsill or in the shared cold frame: tomatoes, chillies, aubergines and early celery. Outdoors from mid March, if the soil is workable: broad beans, onion sets, shallots and first early potatoes.

## Sow in April and May
Outdoors from April: carrots, beetroot, parsnips, peas, spinach, radishes and lettuce, repeating the lettuce and radishes every three weeks. From 15 May, after the frost risk, plant out tomatoes, courgettes, pumpkins, sweetcorn and runner beans.

## Sow in June to August
Sow French beans and beetroot in June, and swedes and kale in June or July. Sow winter radish, turnips and spring cabbage in August. Stop sowing carrots after the end of July, because later sowings rarely size up before the first frost.

## Autumn jobs
Plant garlic and overwintering onion sets in October and November. Sow broad beans and hardy peas in November for an early crop. Cut down spent plants, mulch bare beds with compost, and cover them with a sheet of cardboard against winter rain.
