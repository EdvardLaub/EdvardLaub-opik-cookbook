# Marrow Lane Community Garden: Plot Rules

## Plot allocation
Each household may hold one full plot (3 m by 6 m) or two half plots. Plots are allocated from the waiting list each January, and the list is kept in the order people signed up. A plot not planted by 1 May is offered to the next household on the list.

## Plot upkeep
Keep your plot and the path on its left side free of weeds. Three written reminders from the committee in one season mean the plot is reassigned at the end of October. Bindweed and ground elder must be dug out with the roots, never composted in the shared bins.

## Structures and fences
Raised beds up to 30 cm tall are allowed without asking. Sheds, polytunnels and anything taller than 1.2 m need written approval from the committee. Only the shared tool shed may have a lock.

## Guests and children
Children are welcome when accompanied by an adult plot holder. Guests may water and harvest while the plot holder is away, but may not start new beds. Dogs must stay on a lead and out of the vegetable beds.

## Fees
The annual plot fee is 40 pounds for a full plot and 25 pounds for a half plot, due by 31 January. Water is included. Households on low incomes may ask the treasurer for a reduced fee, in confidence.
