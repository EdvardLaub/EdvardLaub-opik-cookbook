# Marrow Lane Community Garden: Composting

## What goes in the bins
There are three bins by the north hedge: the left bin is for fresh material, the middle bin is turning, and the right bin holds finished compost. Add vegetable peelings, plant trimmings, tea bags, shredded paper and cardboard. Aim for roughly equal volumes of green waste and brown waste.

## What stays out
No cooked food, meat, fish, dairy, cat or dog waste, or diseased plants such as blighted tomatoes and potatoes. Perennial weeds with roots, and any weeds that have gone to seed, go in the green waste skip near the gate instead.

## Turning and collecting
Volunteers turn the middle bin on the first Saturday of every month at 10 am. Finished compost from the right bin is free to all plot holders, up to two buckets per plot each month. Please bring your own buckets and leave the bin lids closed.

## Leaf mould
Leaves are collected in a separate wire cage beside the bins from October to December. Leaf mould needs about a year to rot down, and is used on the fruit bushes and as a mulch.
