# Marrow Lane Community Garden: Watering Rota

## Water butts and the tap
There are six water butts along the east fence, filled from the shed roof. Use butt water first. The mains tap by the gate is switched on from 1 April to 30 September and may only be used with a watering can, never a hose.

## Hose ban and sprinklers
Hoses and sprinklers are banned all year, because the garden shares one small mains connection with the neighbouring school. Seedlings and new transplants may be watered from a can at any time of day.

## Summer rota
From 1 June to 31 August one household waters the shared herb bed and the fruit cage each day. The rota is pinned inside the shed door and rotates every Monday. If you cannot do your day, swap with another household and tell the rota coordinator by message. Do not simply skip it.

## Best time to water
Water in the early morning or after 7 pm, soaking the soil rather than the leaves. During a heatwave, with temperatures above 28 degrees for three days, the committee allows a second can per plot in the evening.
